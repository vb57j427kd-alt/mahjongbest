Our Story

Crafting Excellence Since 2018

Mahjong Best was born at a mahjong table. After years of playing with sets that felt disposable — thin tiles, wobbly racks, fading prints — we decided to build something better.

We partnered with a family-run workshop with three generations of tile-making heritage. Together, we engineered every component — from tile weight distribution to rack ergonomics — to meet the exacting standards of American tournament play.

Today, Mahjong Best sets are played in clubs across 38 states and have been the official set at four national tournaments. But we measure success differently: a Mahjong Best set should still be in play 20 years from now.
